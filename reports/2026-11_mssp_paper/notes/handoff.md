## Storyline (agreed 25 Nov)

Part I, numerical: the differentiable design loop and the illumination criterion (no true model, no extra data);
2-D acoustic study, illumination 16.7 % vs oracle 18.6 % over the fixed layout (10/10 seeds) -> taken to the lab.
Part II, laboratory: 10 mm PMMA plate, 3-D LDV, five features (D1, D2, D4 blind holes, D3 through-hole, M1 glued cube;
D4 is the hardest and separates strategies), blind processing, Mindlin plate model; every strategy replayed on one dense scan.

Backbone: three comparisons from relaxed to strict (Fig. 7, funnel), gain shrinking as constraints are added:
1. **Case (i), moving array, equal inversion cost**: 30 sensors moved 4 times along the gradient vs static 30 points:
   about +1 feature, finds all five far more often. Random moves gain little (designed moves sweep coherently).
   Moves planned beforehand on a best-case model (calibrated on the full defect scan) match the adaptive ones ->
   wording: **"the adaptive design works, and matches a best-case design planned beforehand without needing one"**.
   ~145 points, everything inverted in one pass: visited positions competitive with a uniform layout of the same size
   (D4 better at 4 shots, noisier background).
2. **Case (ii), equal scan budget (30 points)**: adaptive points re-inverted once: +0.3 to +0.6 at a fixed
   false-indication rate; a 30-point design made beforehand gives no gain (except 4 shots).
3. **Case (iii), equal scan and inversion**: direction holds at 4 and 2 shots, not significant; only adaptive finds all
   five (3/14 vs 0/14); the staged inversion costs part of the gain.

Why adaptive rather than beforehand (user): planning beforehand needs a calibrated model and a design run per specimen
and geometry. Discussion: irregular geometries where a grid does not fit; dense uniform scans are the ceiling when
scanning is cheap. Decided NOT to do: fixed-heading (straight-sweep) control, further variants (deadline).
Pre-registered vs exploratory: the fixed false-indication-rate rule and the case (i)/(ii) comparisons against static are
exploratory; say so. Background for that rule in practice: a scan of a defect-free plate (goes in Sec. 6).
